My dearest Tilewa,

If I asked how are you, you'd probably say "I am fine" or at least "I am just tired". But we can skip that part, cause I am not asking for you to brief it or sugarcoat, I am asking because I love you and I care for you and I want to hear it all — yes I do; even as little as it may seem, as little as it may seem to others, because I am not others. So, I ask you Tilewa, how are you?

How are you doing — actually doing? What's on your chest, talk to me. How are you feeling? Your mind, your heart, your emotions… how have you been — truly.?

I'm here for you, always. And I don't want you to carry it alone, I don't want you to go silent about your problems, because I truly care.

I know it might not yet feel that way that I truly care about what bothers you, but I do care — genuinely — and I know I might not show it the best, but I'm trying, and truly I care. So I don't want you to keep what bothers you to yourself, because I am here for you — Daniel is here.

I love you, I love us, and I choose us everyday. And I hope as you go through the moments and photos and memories, you feel how much I love you, how much I care for you, and how much I cherish every moment with you. I love you, Tilewa.

Seeing you smile makes my day brighter, and I want you to wake up and feel the love I have for you every single day. I love you, Tilewa. Have the most wonderful day!

— Always yours 🤍
